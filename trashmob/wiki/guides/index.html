Trash Mob guides

Short guides to how the game works. The wiki's tables (jobs, items, recipes, unlocks) have the exact numbers; these explain the why.

  • Your first hour: getting started, reading a job, keeping your slots busy.
  • Heat and the Hot Streak: Heat, Toll's board, the Stiffs, and when to play it hot.
  • The Pound: how you end up in jail, and the four ways out.
  • Contracts: the neighbors, their asks, and Markers.
  • Heists: stages, banking, intel, and walking out.
  • Fair play: Shinies, the busted TV, the Supporter Pack, and what's never for sale.

Everything in the game also has an entry in the Case File (More, at the bottom of the Street), with the current numbers.

These guides don't cover the late-night turn. That part you find yourself.
