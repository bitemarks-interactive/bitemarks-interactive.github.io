Heists

Heists are the big jobs: a building, a plan, and several stages between you and the good stuff. As the badger puts it: walking out is the whole trick.

The heists

  • The Gas-N-Go Back Room: a two-stage practice run. It teaches the basics.
  • Catch-A-Critter HQ: the first real one. How far you get here opens Downtown.
  • The First Raccoon National Bank: the big one.

How a heist works

A heist is a run of stages. At each stage you roll for success, with odds built from:

  • your skills;
  • your crew: crows in the right roles;
  • supplies you bring along;
  • the gear you're wearing.

After each stage you choose:

  • Bank it: walk out with everything you've taken so far.
  • Push on: the next stage pays more, and the odds get harder.

If a stage busts, you lose what that run hadn't banked, and the crows you brought lie low for a while. That's all: your levels, your gear and your earlier banked hauls are untouched.

Intel

Intel is what you spend to start a heist: no intel, no heist. You get it from:

  • casing jobs;
  • Hacking;
  • crows on stakeout duty;
  • some contracts (Goldie is very interested);
  • the busted TV, if you watch its channel.

No clock

A heist waits for you. Run a stage, close the app, come back tomorrow: it's exactly where you left it. A heist works just as well across a few short visits.

Why bother

  • The best loot is here. Unique gear, the one-of-a-kind pieces, only drops on heist stages. (Skill capes are unique too, but you earn those by leveling.)
  • Banking stages opens things. Downtown wants Catch-A-Critter HQ banked to stage 3.
  • Ranks lean on heists in the mid-game.

Tips

  • Bank early while you learn a heist's stages.
  • Check your weakest skill for the next stage before you push.
  • Stock up on intel before you try the bank: you can't start without it.
