Your first hour

You're a raccoon with a bin and a plan. Here's how to turn that into an empire.

Jobs and slots

Everything you do is a job: rummaging a dumpster, picking a lock, burning down a pile of old yard signs. A job takes a few seconds, gives XP in its skill, and usually makes something. Once started, it repeats until you stop it or it runs out of what it needs.

You have job slots, shown in the strip at the top of every screen. Keep them busy. That's most of the game.

To start a job, open the Map, pick a place, and tap a job. Or open Skills, pick a skill, and go to where it trains.

Reading a job card

Every card tells you:

  • how long one run takes;
  • the XP it gives;
  • what it needs (supplies, tools, other skills);
  • what it makes, and the odds for anything that isn't certain;
  • its success chance, if it has a check.

A locked job shows everything it needs, ticked or crossed, with where you are now. If something's locked, the card tells you exactly why.

Checks

Riskier jobs roll a check. Your weakest skill on the job decides the odds, so a great Locks level won't save you if your Stealth is behind. When a run fails, it tells you which skill let you down.

Gear raises your levels for checks. Craft it with Tinkering, Tailoring and Chemistry, and wear it in the Stash's Outfit.

The crit tap

When a job's bar reaches its striped end, tap it for a crit: a little extra. It's a bonus for paying attention, never a requirement. Idle play is fine.

The early loop

  1. Scavenging turns up trash and materials.
  2. Crafting skills turn materials into tools and gear.
  3. Thieving and Burglary bring in loot and dirty cash.
  4. The badger at the Pawn & Loan buys your loot.
  5. Laundering turns dirty cash into clean cash, which the bigger unlocks want.

Skills feed each other all the way up, so a skill you've ignored is usually the one holding something back.

Heat

Crimes leave Evidence behind, and Evidence turns into Heat over time. Heat cools on its own. For now, just keep an eye on the bar. The Heat guide explains why you might want more of it later.

While you're away

The game keeps running while the app is closed. When you come back, the morning paper tells you what your raccoon got up to.

What next?

The Street always shows one useful next step, and the rank tracker shows what your next rank needs. If you're stuck, start there.

Quick tips

  • Keep every slot busy. If a slot runs out of supplies, it switches to a job it can still run.
  • Tap the cop car on the Street to duck: a little less Heat.
  • Read the Case File. Every system has an entry, with the real numbers.
