Fair play: how money works in Trash Mob

Trash Mob is free, and it's meant to stay fair. Here's exactly what's for sale and what never will be.

Shinies

Shinies are the game's special currency. You get them by:

  • playing: total-level milestones pay them out, and a lucky find drops one now and then;
  • codes: posted on our Discord and socials;
  • buying a pack, if you want to.

Shinies only buy unlocks a free player can earn by playing. They speed some things up; they never put you ahead of anyone.

Never for sale

Not for money, not for Shinies, not for watching ads:

  • skill levels or XP;
  • Heat removal;
  • protection from hits, cures for a concussion, or streak insurance;
  • bail or a way out of the Pound;
  • anything in the late-night turn the game saves for the brave.

No energy. No timers to pay off. No loot boxes.

The busted TV (ads)

The busted TV in your Hideout plays infomercials, which are ads from Google. It's off until you turn it on, and while it's off, the game never talks to an ad network.

  • Every channel says what it gives before you watch: Sugar Rush (jobs twice as fast for a while), an envelope that pays twice, intel for a heist, a few Shinies, or more of your overnight haul.
  • You choose when to watch. Nothing interrupts you.
  • Each channel has a daily limit. There's nothing on during your first day or while you're in the Pound, and no heist intel is offered mid-heist.

The Supporter Pack

A one-time purchase. It gives you every TV bonus without watching the TV, under the same daily limits. Never more than an ad-watcher gets, just without the ads.

Trash Mob is made by one person at Bitemarks Interactive. If you like it and want to help, the Supporter Pack is the way.

Your data

  • Cloud saves are optional, and back up your raccoons.
  • "Count me on the sign" (in Settings) sends the day you last played, once a day, so the town's population sign can count who's still around. Turn it off any time; that also deletes the date already stored.
  • Delete my cloud data in Settings removes everything of yours from the cloud.

The full privacy policy is at bitemarksinteractive.com/privacy.html.
