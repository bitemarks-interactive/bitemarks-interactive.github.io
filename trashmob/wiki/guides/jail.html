The Pound

Animal Control's Pound is where raccoons go when Toll gets lucky. It's a setback, never a loss.

How you end up there

Only two ways:

  1. Toll raids you while you're Hot or Scorching and you're in the game.
  2. You tap "Keep going" on a crime slot that paused one job short of the Heat cap. That one's your gamble.

Nothing else jails you. While you're away, it can't happen at all.

Your sentence

  • The first one is short. Each prior doubles the next, up to a cap.
  • Priors clear as you stay out of trouble.
  • The jail screen always shows your time left and every way out.
  • The sentence keeps running while you're away.

While you're inside

Your free jobs wait and pick up where they stopped. Meanwhile every slot runs a jail job:

  • Safe jobs (Laundry Duty, Cook Duty, Stamp License Plates) knock time off your sentence, up to half of it.
  • Middle jobs earn small trade goods.
  • Rough jobs add time but earn Clout, respect that only counts inside. Clout carries over between sentences.

Jail jobs always pay less than free ones. Jail is never better than being out.

Four ways out

  1. Wait it out. Nothing is lost.
  2. Post bail with dirty cash, or call in Markers for the badger's lawyer. Bail is supposed to hurt.
  3. Start a riot. It costs Clout. Win and you walk out now. Lose and it's solitary: more time, and no jail jobs for a while.
  4. Crew breakout. It needs enough fed crows. Win and you're out, but at Scorching Heat as a fugitive, with a short grace period before any raid. Lose and you get more time, and some crows lie low for a few hours.

You leave at Cool Heat every time, except after a breakout.

Shinies never buy a way out. Nothing in the store does.

Tips

  • Run the safe jobs first: they shorten the stay.
  • Save Clout for a riot when the sentence is long.
  • A breakout leaves you Scorching. Cool off before you start crimes again.
