Contracts

Word gets around. The neighbors have things they'd like done, and they'll pay.

How it works

  • The Contracts board lists asks from the neighborhood's clients. Each has a goal, a reward and a deadline in days.
  • You complete a contract by playing normally: the jobs that count toward it count whether you're watching or away.
  • Miss the deadline and you just lose that contract. Nothing else happens.
  • Some of the pay is Markers: favors owed to you.

Markers

Spend Markers at the badger's counter on things you can't get any other way. You can also call one in:

  • during a possum hit, to make it go away;
  • in the Pound, for the badger's lawyer instead of bail money.

Markers only come from contracts. They're never for sale.

The clients

  • The badger. His first contracts are lessons: laundering, your first racket, your first heist. Do them first.
  • Mrs. Feldman. Pearls, curlers, grudges. She'd like her neighbors to have slightly worse lives.
  • Duchess. Royalty of the storm drain, or so she says. She speaks for Us.
  • Kevin. HOA middle management. Every ask is an action item.
  • Linda. Recently divorced. Her ex got the good couch, and she'd like it back.
  • Goldie. A fence with a gold chain and a lot of opinions about the bank.

Tips

  • Take contracts that fit what your slots are already doing: free rewards.
  • Keep a few Markers in your pocket. A hit at the wrong moment is cheaper to buy off than to lose.
