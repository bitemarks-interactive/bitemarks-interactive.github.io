Heat and the Hot Streak

Heat is how much attention you're getting. Most games make you avoid it. Trash Mob pays you to ride it, if you're careful.

How Heat works

  • Crimes leave Evidence at the scene. As Evidence fades, it turns into Heat.
  • Heat cools on its own, quickly once you stop committing crimes.
  • Cleanup jobs clear Evidence before it turns into Heat. Think of Cleanup as the dial for how fast you heat up.
  • Ducking a cop car on the Street takes a little Heat off.

The Heat bar has four zones: Cool, Warm, Hot and Scorching. A pointer shows where your Heat is headed from the jobs already running.

The Hot Streak

Any job that finishes while you're Warm or hotter earns extra XP. The hotter the zone, the bigger the multiplier. The extra doesn't land right away: it builds up in your streak, shown under the Heat bar.

  • The streak pays out to the skills that earned it when Heat drops back to Cool, however it gets there.
  • You lose the streak if Toll raids you while you're Hot or Scorching, or if you lose a possum hit.
  • It only grows while you're playing. While you're away, nothing can take it, and it banks itself when Heat cools.
  • It stacks with Sugar Rush, the busted TV's speed boost.

The XP and loot you've already earned are never at risk. Only the unbanked extra is.

Toll's board

Detective Toll keeps a board. Part of every bit of fading Evidence goes on it as a pin.

  • When the board fills (15 pins), he raids. If you're Hot or Scorching in the game at that moment, it's the Pound. Otherwise the raid just piles on Heat and stops short of the cap.
  • If a raid comes due while you're playing, he gives you a few minutes' warning first.
  • His car parks closer on the Street as the case builds.

Ways to keep the board thin:

  • clean up before Evidence fades, so no pin is ever filed;
  • Burn the Board at Precinct 41;
  • put crows on Pin Lifters;
  • bribe him from the Board;
  • tip him off about a possum.

The Stiffs

While you're playing and Hot, a gang of possums may try a hit, more often at Scorching. You get a short warning and five choices, with your odds shown:

  • Fight (Brawling and Marksmanship, plus any guns in your crew's kit);
  • Run (Climbing and Getaway Driving);
  • Hide (Stealth);
  • Pay them off in dirty cash;
  • Call in a Marker.

Win and you keep your streak, sometimes with a trophy. Lose and the streak is gone and you're concussed for a while, at half XP. They never hit you while you're away, and a hit that's been announced still happens if you close the app.

Jail is never an accident

Only two things send you to the Pound: a raid while you're Hot or Scorching, or choosing Keep going when a crime slot pauses one job short of the cap. Anything else that would hit the cap stops just short of it. See The Pound.

Ride it or stay cool?

Both are fine.

  • Staying cool is steady and safe.
  • Riding the Heat is faster and riskier. Build a streak, watch the board, cash out before he cashes in, and keep your defense skills up for the possums.

Check-in players get the gentle version automatically: while you're away, Heat parks at the top of Hot and nothing can take your streak.
