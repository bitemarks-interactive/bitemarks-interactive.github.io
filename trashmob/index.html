Trash Mob

An idle crime game about a raccoon working his way from dumpster mischief to crime boss.

Coming soon to Google Play Support

Rummage, tinker, pick locks and case the neighborhood. Set your raccoon to work, close the app, and come back to see what he got up to. Level more than twenty skills, from Scavenging to Laundering, hire a crew of crows, claim Turf from the possums, and plan heists on the people who have it coming: the exterminator, Animal Control, the HOA.

Keep an eye on the Heat. Leave too much Evidence around and you'll spend the afternoon in the Pound.
