Delete your account and data

For Trash Mob, by Bitemarks Interactive.

Right now: there's nothing on our side to delete

Trash Mob doesn't have accounts yet, and it keeps your save only on your phone. To delete everything, uninstall the game, or open Android's Settings → Apps → Trash Mob → Storage → Clear storage.

When cloud saves arrive

Takes effect when cloud saves ship

A future update adds cloud saves, so your progress can follow you to a new phone. From then on, Trash Mob will store an anonymous player ID, your cloud save, your purchase history, and, if you choose to link one, your Google account's email address and name. You'll be able to delete it any time, in either of these ways:

In the game

  1. Open Trash Mob and tap Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete my cloud data.
  3. Confirm. Your cloud data is deleted, and the game carries on with the save on your phone only.

Without the app

  1. Email support@bitemarksinteractive.com with the subject "Delete my Trash Mob data".
  2. Include your player ID (in the game under Settings → Account) or the Google account you linked, so we can find your data.
  3. We'll confirm by email, and delete it within 30 days.
