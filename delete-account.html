Delete your account and data

For Trash Mob, by Bitemarks Interactive.

Two kinds of data

Your save lives on your phone. If you back it up, a copy goes to the cloud under an anonymous player ID, and that copy is the part we can delete for you. To clear the copy on your phone, uninstall the game or open Android's Settings → Apps → Trash Mob → Storage → Clear storage.

What we hold

An anonymous player ID, your cloud saves and their names, the codes you redeemed, the date you last played (for the town's population sign), and your purchase history. We never collect your name or email. More detail is in the privacy policy.

In the game

  1. Open Trash Mob and tap Settings.
  2. Under Cloud saves, tap Delete my cloud data.
  3. Confirm. Your cloud data is deleted, and the game carries on with the save on your phone only.

Without the app

  1. Email support@bitemarksinteractive.com with the subject "Delete my Trash Mob data".
  2. Tell us what you'd like deleted. Your data is tied to an anonymous player ID, not your name, so the in-game button is the surest way; we'll do what we can for the rest.
  3. We'll confirm by email, and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted
