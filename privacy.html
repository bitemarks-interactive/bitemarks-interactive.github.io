Trash Mob Privacy Policy

Bitemarks Interactive · Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to Trash Mob 0.9 and later, until cloud saves ship

Short version: Trash Mob doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data. Your game save lives on your phone. Notifications are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app collects

Nothing. Trash Mob has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It doesn't send your game progress, device identifiers or any other information to us or to anyone else, and it doesn't use your phone's advertising ID.

Your game save

Your progress is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We can't see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you've turned on Android's backup (for example Google One backup, or moving data to a new phone), Android may include the app's save in that backup. That backup is handled by Google or your device maker under your own account and settings; we don't receive it and can't access it.

Some things in the game happen once a day by your phone's clock. The game reads the clock on your phone; the time never leaves your device.

Notifications

The game can remind you when your idle progress has hit its limit. These reminders are local notifications: the app schedules them on your device, and no server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in Android's settings for the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminder above. Optional; the game works without it.
  • Run at startup and prevent phone from sleeping: used by the notification system to keep a scheduled reminder after your phone restarts and to deliver it on time.
  • Network access: included by the app framework. The game doesn't use it to send anything.

Children

Trash Mob is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

There's nothing on our side to delete: everything lives on your device. Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android's settings, and your save is gone.

Changes

When we add features that handle data, such as cloud saves or in-app purchases, we'll update this policy before those features ship and change the date above.
