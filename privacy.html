Trash Mob Privacy Policy

Bitemarks Interactive · Effective 31 October 2026

Short version: to keep your save in the cloud, Trash Mob gives you an anonymous player ID. If you back up, your save goes to the cloud; to move it to a new phone, the game gives you a one-time code. If you buy something, the purchase is recorded so it can be restored. Ads are optional: only if you switch on the in-game TV does Google's ad service run, and then it collects what it needs to show ads. No analytics, nothing sold, and you can delete it all.

Who we are

Trash Mob is made by Bitemarks Interactive. Contact: support@bitemarksinteractive.com.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, files, messages, browsing history or payment card details. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card.

Optional ads

Trash Mob shows ads only when you ask for one: a busted TV in the Hideout plays short video ads ("infomercials"), and you can watch one for an in-game bonus. There are no banners and no ads you didn't choose. The TV is off until you turn it on, and until then the ad service isn't started and nothing is sent to it. Where no consent form applies, turning the TV on is your agreement to see ads, which are personalized only as described below.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent (through Google's consent form) when you turn the TV on. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Personalized ads only for adults who agree: the TV shows personalized ads only to players Google Play confirms are 18 or over, and only with their consent. Everyone else, including anyone whose age we don't know, gets non-personalized ads (ads that aren't based on your interests or activity in other apps). All ads are limited to content suitable for teens, and gambling ads are blocked.
  • Turning it off: turn the TV off in the Hideout or in Settings, and the ad service won't be started again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying the Supporter Pack gives you the TV's bonuses without watching ads and doesn't start the ad service.

What we don't do

  • No ads you didn't ask for, and nothing from the ad service unless you turn the TV on.
  • No analytics and no tracking of you by us, in this app or across other apps or websites. The date you last played is used only for the population sign's count.
  • The population sign: the town's sign shows how many players were active in the last 30 days. We count that on our server from the date each anonymous player last played; only the total is ever shown or shared, never anything about you. You can leave yourself out: Settings → Count me on the sign.
  • We don't sell your data. The only data shared for advertising is what Google's ad service collects itself while the TV is on, as described above.

Who processes it for us

We use these services to run cloud saves and purchases. They process data on our behalf, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, cloud save storage, and the server that checks redeem codes): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (payments): policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (keeps track of your purchases so they can be restored): revenuecat.com/privacy

Google's ad service (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) collects and uses data under Google's own policies when the TV is on: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and policies.google.com/privacy.

These services may store data on servers in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

  • Your player ID, cloud saves and last-played date: for as long as you keep playing. If an account with no purchases isn't used for 24 months, we delete it.
  • Move codes: until used, or a day.
  • Purchase history: as long as needed to restore your purchases and handle refunds, and as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of sales, even after you delete your account.
  • Data Google's ad service collects: kept by Google under its own retention policies; we don't receive it in a form that identifies you.
  • After you ask us to delete your data: within 30 days, except the purchase records the law requires us to keep.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and cloud save at any time:

  • In the game: Settings → Cloud saves → Delete my cloud data.
  • On the web, without the app: follow the steps at https://bitemarksinteractive.com/delete-account.html.

Deleting removes your cloud saves, your profile, the codes you redeemed, the date you last played and your anonymous player ID. The save on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to support@bitemarksinteractive.com. Your data is tied to an anonymous player ID rather than your name, so the fastest route is the in-game button (Settings → Cloud saves → Delete my cloud data); for anything else, write to us and we'll do what we can. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA; we honor them for everyone.

Children

Trash Mob is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are never directed at children, and only players Google Play confirms as adults can get personalized ads (see Optional ads). If you believe a child under 13 has backed up a save or made a purchase, write to us and we'll delete the data.

Security

Data travels to our providers encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on their secured infrastructure. Only the player who owns a cloud save can read it.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we'll update this policy before the change ships, change the date above, and say so in the game.
